Sectors which produce a single emissions estimate for the whole period are now stored in the output without a time dimension, instead of repeating the same estimate for every time step. The `ch4_sector_*` variables therefore have either `(time, vertical, y, x)` or `(vertical, y, x)` dimensions depending on whether the sector resolves emissions per time step, and which sectors fall into each group can vary with the length of the period being processed. Consumers reading individual sectors should check the dimensions present rather than assuming a time dimension. The `ch4_total` and `OCH4_TOTAL` variables are unchanged, and always include a time dimension.
